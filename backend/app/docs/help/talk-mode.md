# Talk Mode (hands-free voice)

Talk Mode is a full-screen, call-style voice conversation: you speak, the
assistant answers out loud, no buttons needed between turns.

## How do I start Talk Mode?
Press the **Talk** button next to the chat input. The conversation continues
in the same chat — everything said is transcribed into it.

## How does a turn work?
Speak naturally; when you pause, your speech is transcribed and sent. The
reply streams back as speech (sentence by sentence) with live captions on
screen.

## Can I interrupt the assistant?
Yes — just start talking while it's speaking (barge-in). Playback stops and
your new speech becomes the next message.

## How do I mute or end the call?
Use the on-screen **Mute** button to stop listening temporarily and
**End call** to leave Talk Mode. Your conversation stays in the chat.

## Which voice does it use?
The voice and speaking speed come from Settings → Voice.
