# Listening to a message

## How do I hear a message aloud?
On Android or desktop, tap **Listen** on a message. Its button changes to show
when speech is preparing, playing, paused, buffering, finished, or failed.
Longer messages may need a moment to prepare their first paragraph.
Listen and voice previews are currently unavailable in the web app.

## How do I control playback?
Tap the active message's speaking button to pause or resume. Tap the small
circular speed button beside it to cycle playback speed; the voice is not
regenerated. The adjacent **More** menu shows paragraph progress and has
**Stop** plus previous/next prepared paragraph. Stop is available while speech
is preparing or buffering. If the message scrolls out of view, scroll back to
its button for controls. Starting another message stops the current one first.

## What happens if I leave the app?
On Android, read-aloud pauses when Garbanzo AI leaves the foreground. Returning
to the app keeps your place; tap **Resume** when you want to continue. There
is no background audio or lock-screen control.

## What if speech stops or fails?
The active message's **More** menu shows the error if the connection or speech
worker fails. Tap **Retry from this paragraph** there, or tap the message's
**Retry** button, after the connection returns. A cancelled or
expired session is distinct from a finished message; replaying an expired
session starts a new one.

## Which voices can I use?
The initial curated voices are Alba for English and Lola for Spain Spanish.
On Android or desktop, you can preview each voice and set a separate default
for each language in Voice settings. Automatic language mode selects the voice
by paragraph; a language override lets you keep one language for the whole
message. Read-aloud voice settings do not change Talk Mode.
