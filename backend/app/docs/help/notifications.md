# Notifications

## Where do I see my notifications?
The bell icon in the app bar shows an unread count; tap it for the
notifications page. **Mark all read** clears the badge.

## When do I get notified?
- A reply finished after you left the conversation or closed the app
- Activity in rooms you're a member of
- Scheduled actions that ran for you

## How do I get push notifications on my phone?
Install the Android app and log in — the device registers itself
automatically. Push also works on web browsers that allow notifications.

## How do I control which notifications I get?
Settings → Notifications has per-category preferences (chat replies, rooms,
scheduled actions).

## How do I silence one conversation or room?
Mute it from its own menu — pick a duration or forever. Muting stops
notifications for just that conversation/room without turning anything else
off. See the Chat and Rooms guides.
