# Micro-apps

Micro-apps are small web apps the assistant can build and change for you by
chatting — a habit tracker, a countdown, a family dashboard — shown in a
panel beside the chat.

## How do I create or open a micro-app?
Ask for it in chat ("make me a packing checklist app"). The assistant uses
its micro-app tool; the app opens in the side panel. Reopen a closed panel
with **Reopen the micro-app panel**.

## How do I change a micro-app?
Ask in chat ("add a dark theme", "sort items by date"). The assistant edits
the app's code in a workspace; the panel reloads with the result.

## How do I publish a micro-app?
Press **Publish changes** in the panel (optionally with a commit message) —
changes are committed and deployed. **Revert all changes?** discards
unpublished edits.
